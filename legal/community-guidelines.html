Welcome to MyriadFlow! To ensure a positive experience for everyone, please follow these guidelines:

01Be Respectful

Do

Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Communicate thoughtfully.

Don’t

Engage in harassment, hate speech, or offensive language.

02Protect Intellectual Property

Do

Share content you own or have permission to use. Give proper credit.

Don’t

Upload or claim content that isn’t yours or infringe on others’ rights.

03Follow Platform Rules

Do

Adhere to MyriadFlow’s Terms of Service and Creator Terms. Report inappropriate behavior.

Don’t

Break platform rules or use it for illegal activities.

04Ensure Accuracy

Do

Provide truthful information about your content and transactions.

Don’t

Mislead others or make false claims.

05Respect Privacy

Do

Protect others’ personal information and seek permission before sharing.

Don’t

Share personal details without consent or invade privacy.

06Promote Positivity

Do

Engage positively and supportively. Offer and accept constructive feedback.

Don’t

Troll or create negativity. Attack or undermine the community.

07Reporting Issues

Report any violations to our support team. We take issues seriously and will act as needed. You can contact us at contact@myriadflow.com.

Thank you for helping us keep MyriadFlow a great place for everyone!
