## Who we are

MyriadFlow LLC builds **MyriadFlow**, the vibe manufacturing launchpad — a platform where
anyone with a hardware idea can develop a prototype, show it to a community, raise support
for a production run, and bring the finished product to buyers. **TVASTR**, our
manufacturing intelligence engine, turns a brief into specifications, parametric design,
feasibility checks, prototype jobs and a production handoff. **Phygitals** give a physical
product a verifiable digital life through a FlowTag and a Product Passport.

The platform is in private beta. New projects are set up by the team, in
[our Discord](https://discord.gg/38jktRtuY7).
