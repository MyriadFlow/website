01 / How it works

Three steps, one tap.

  1. 01TagA FlowTag — an NFC chip with its own key — is fitted into or onto the product.
  2. 02TapAny modern phone reads it. No app, no account, no scanning the right corner.
  3. 03VerifyThe tag signs that single tap, so a copied link can't pass as the real object.

02 / The Product Passport

Everything about the object, one tap away.

The passport is the object's own page: what it is, who made it, what went into it, and everything that has happened to it since. It travels with the product, not with the receipt.

  • MakerStudio, workshop or factory, and the people behind it.
  • MaterialsWhat it's made of, down to the part numbers that matter.
  • EditionWhich one of how many, and when it was made.
  • StoryProcess notes, photos and the decisions along the way.
  • OwnershipWho owns it now, and the transfers before that.
  • Digital twinThe 3D model of the object itself.

Optional · Extended Passport. Warranty, repair log, firmware history and resale terms can ride along when a product needs them.

05 / Beyond the QR code

QR code opens it. FlowTag proves it.
